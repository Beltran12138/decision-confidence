EVIDENCE-ADJUSTED LEADERBOARD · No. 1 · OCTOBER 2026

Public AI trading leaderboards rank by return. None of them can yet name a winner.

We take the equity history each board publishes and ask a different question of the same data: how much can the ranking tell you, and how long until it could first show that the top entry is skilled rather than lucky?

t = annualised Sharpe × √years. One result needs t ≥ 2. Picking the best of N entries raises the bar: best of 41 needs about 3.26, best of 23,376 about 4.76. How this is computed.

Summary

* Months of clean, forward history the top entry would need to clear the best-of-N bar, if its true Sharpe were the value shown. Counting every entry as an independent attempt overstates the charge when entries are correlated; the true Sharpe of any entry is unknown. Both cut in opposite directions.

What would bring the date forward

Longer windowsA month of live trading is too short for any realistic Sharpe. Carrying Phase I records into Phase II doubles each team's evidence for free.
Publish the historyRank only entries with a full equity curve. A single snapshot supports no Sharpe, no drawdown and no test.
Group the variantsParameter sweeps from one owner are one attempt with many tries. Show them as a family with one bar, not as separate winners.
